Privacy Policy

Ratzon operates the Ratzon app and ratzonapp.com. Questions about this policy can be sent to jared@ratzonapp.com.

Information we collect

We collect the account information you provide: name, email address, phone number, birthday, school, tradition, optional community membership, and account timezone. We also collect photos and captions you submit as wrap check-ins, contribution and donation records, device/app diagnostics needed to operate the service, and reports you submit about community content.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure your account, operate check-ins and the community feed, provide account support, administer contributions and donations, prevent abuse and fraud, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing and payments

Wrap photos and captions you choose to share appear in the Global feed for signed-in Ratzon members. The My Community feed filters these posts to members of your selected community. If you join a community, that community’s designated admins can view your profile, complete wrap history, captions, and wrap photos, including check-ins you do not share to the feed, for challenge administration and support. The app discloses this access when you join. Payments are processed by Stripe and Apple Pay; Ratzon does not receive or store complete payment-card numbers. We may share information with service providers that process payments, host data, or help operate the app, only as needed for those purposes.

Retention and deletion

You can start account deletion in the app under Account → Settings → Delete account. We delete your account profile, onboarding information, posts, and stored check-in photos. We cancel an active recurring contribution before deletion. We may retain the minimum payment, fraud, tax, or other records required by law, and will explain any delay or legal retention requirement if it applies.

Community safety

You can report a community post or block its author using the post’s options menu. Blocking hides that person’s posts from your feeds. We review reports and may remove posts or restrict accounts that violate our community rules. For safety or privacy concerns, contact us at the email above.

Children

Ratzon is not available to children under 13. Account setup requires confirmation that the member is at least 13, and a provided birthday indicating an age under 13 is rejected.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The current version will always be available at this address.
