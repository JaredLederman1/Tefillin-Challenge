# Ratzon design preferences

- Across the entire platform, never use a large title followed by a smaller subtitle, tagline, or explanatory sentence. This applies to signup, login, app screens, cards, dialogs, and the landing page.
- Use a single concise heading or field label, followed directly by the relevant controls or content. For example: “Full name” and the input; never “Full name” followed by “Let’s make this yours.”
- Do not add decorative or motivational helper copy. Essential optional markers, input formats, validation errors, progress, and account/payment status belong with their controls or in standalone notices.
- Preserve required explanations (such as Chol Hamoed customs and streak protection) as standalone text, without a separate title/subtitle pairing.
- Apply this preference to all future UI work unless the user explicitly changes it.

- Explicit user-approved exception: show the slogan “Live With Intention” beneath the Ratzon logo on the app welcome screen and website. Keep the no-subtext rule everywhere else.
- Explicit user-approved completion sequence: after signup Finish, show a white screen with the Ratzon logo animation and “Welcome to Ratzon”, then fade the entire white welcome screen out to reveal Today. Do not use a panel wipe.

- Main app headers show a left-aligned bold page title. Today shows the full date including year, horizontally centered. The transparent R logo belongs inside the raised center Today navigation button, replacing the sun icon. Do not duplicate the logo or title in the header.

- Vertically center labels, titles, dates, captions, icons, and selection indicators within their rows and controls. Use balanced vertical spacing and avoid font padding that makes text look off-center. Retain the specified horizontal alignment and page layout.
- Birthday month, day, and year wheels provide a subtle selection haptic when user scrolling changes the selected increment. Avoid feedback for initial positioning or automatic date corrections.

- Bottom navigation has exactly three tabs: Community, Today (raised center R), and You. Charity voting is a popup only; You does not reference donations, voting, or community impact. There are no personal monetary wallets or separate Challenge or Wallet tabs.

- Apple signup reuses the name supplied by Apple and skips the name prompt, including when Apple withholds a returning user's name. Signup asks Phone Number, School (skip allowed), Birthday (required for the 13+ age check), Tradition, and whether the member owns tefillin. Do not ask gender. Non-owners see guidance to contact their nearest Chabad center on campus or in their local community to arrange tefillin to use. Do not collect a borrowing choice or offer tefillin purchases. Do not ask religiosity.
- You shows compact personal information, community, membership, and account settings. Voting does not allocate a personal balance.
- Charity voting opens as a congratulations popup only on the first of the month in New York time for active subscribers who completed every required wrap day of the previous full calendar month. Exempt days do not count; pending reviews must be approved. No voting entry point belongs in You. The requested congratulations wording is an explicit exception to the no-motivational-copy preference. Subscription app access remains independent of wrapping completion.
- Show actual recorded company funds available for the voting round only in the popup. Update the shared total from reconciled accounting records; never calculate it as $1.80 times subscribers. Unknown amounts show awaiting reconciliation, while a recorded zero displays $0.00.
- Membership is a standard App Store subscription for ongoing digital app access, independent of check-in completion. Ratzon donates all positive monthly net proceeds after documented fees, refunds, applicable taxes, and operating costs. The membership screen states a target of $1.80 donated per member each month, clearly distinguished from a guarantee and the localized subscription charge. Monthly qualified member voting selects the charity recipient. Do not promise a fixed donation per subscription or create member earnings, cash-valued tokens, redistribution, or tefillin savings goals.
